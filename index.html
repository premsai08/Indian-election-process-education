How it works

Indian Election Timeline

  1. Electoral rolls are prepared and revised for every Assembly Constituency.
  2. ECI announces the election schedule; the Model Code of Conduct starts immediately.
  3. Candidates file nominations before the Returning Officer after notification.
  4. Nominations are scrutinized, withdrawals close, then final candidates are published.
  5. Campaigning runs until the silence period before polling.
  6. Mock poll is conducted at the booth before actual polling begins.
  7. Voters vote at their assigned polling station using EVM and VVPAT.
  8. Sealed EVMs are stored in strong rooms and later counted under ECI rules.
Citizen

Enroll as voter

Use Form 6 if you are an Indian citizen, 18+, and need inclusion in the electoral roll. Use Form 8 for correction, shifting residence, replacement EPIC, or PwD marking.

Candidate

Contest election

File nomination with the Returning Officer, submit affidavit and required documents, pay deposit, attend scrutiny, follow expenditure and MCC rules.

MCC

Election code

The Model Code of Conduct starts from schedule announcement and restricts misuse of office, hate appeals, inducements, public-funded ads, and booth entry.
